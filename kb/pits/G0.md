node. First, `conventions.ownership_rule` is implemented ONCE, in `check-ownership.mjs`, and

**The limitation of this node, stated here because it belongs in the record and not only in a
message.** `--check-tables` compares TABLE DATA. The same G1 defect had a second, disjoint
register: `mcp-6d` swept `erp/**.md` prose and found eleven more sites, four of which asserted
the deleted `G1 -> G3` edge as a live prerequisite, and PM found two more in charters — the
register where a seat actually receives its instructions and which neither instrument read.
**The two registers are disjoint BY CONSTRUCTION, so "the tables are clean" and "the corpus is
clean" are different claims and only the first is mechanised.** PM ruled (D-36) that the second
register gets a node at the SECOND occurrence, not the first: one is a defect cleared by hand,
two is a missing instrument. Anyone citing `--check-tables` as proof the plan is self-consistent
is citing it for more than it says.
