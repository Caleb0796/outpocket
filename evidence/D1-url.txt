Why it matters at all, restated for whoever reads this after the upgrade:
S9's state digest lives in one process's in-memory Map, and S6's TOCTOU
closure is true by construction of a single process. A second instance
(intentional or accidental, e.g. Render's own zero-downtime rolling deploy
briefly running two) would silently reintroduce both — the digest would
diverge between instances and the lock race S6 closes would reopen. Re-check
this file, not just the dashboard's instance count, after the paid-tier
upgrade lands.
