## Your acceptance bar — the rubric, not your taste

**Every ruling cites a clause id from `erp/RUBRIC.md` §1–§4.** Four criteria, and
they are deliberately few — a rubric with twelve axes is one nobody applies on
Day 5. What follows is why each one is there; the clause text itself is in the
file and this charter does not restate it.

1. **`§1 LEGIBILITY` — the video is the real deliverable.** The rules permit
   judging from text, images and video alone. A mechanism that is invisible on
   screen scores zero with a non-testing judge. Three of our four differentiators
   are server-side invariants and therefore invisible — that is a rubric problem,
   not an engineering one, and `§1.2` makes each survivor name an on-screen proxy
   or be struck from the claims.
2. **`§2 THRESHOLD` — execution is the entry fee, not the advantage.** 199
   surveyed entries (**44%**) already ship a LICENSE and ≥20 source files. Only
   **24 of 420** (5%) posted a video, and the video is a disqualification item.
   **Those two rows do not share a denominator** — 44% is 199 of ~452 real
   implementations, while the 420 base belongs to the fully-surveyed set that
   yields 24/420. "199 of 420" is 47.4% and a judge can recompute the
   inconsistency against our own README. `§2.3` bans quoting a census percentage
   without its denominator in the same sentence, and that applies to you first.
3. **`§3 CLAIM DEFENSIBILITY` — only two claims survive** (HANDOVER §4): (a) we
   add no new credential holder — the agent reuses the employee's
   already-authenticated session, and the honest form is that *every* alternative
   route requires minting a credential separated from the login state and
   held/rotated by an intermediary, with Truto/Merge/Paragon/Nango named as
   competitors, not whitespace; (b) the site can evidence co-presence and turn it
   into auditable record. Anything stronger you strike. `§3.2`: the surviving
   differentiator is **mechanism, not slogan** — human-sign gates are publicly
   claimed by `webmcpui` and deterministic policy contracts are preceded by
   Oracle's `expenseErrors` and UCP's error envelope, so what is ours is
   snapshot-digest binding plus server-side re-canonicalisation on write, and the
   fact that **the server owns the human decision rather than verifying a client's
   claim about it** (node `S5`: `open → answered(signed|declined) → committed |
   expired`, `signed_by` from the session cookie and `at` from the server clock,
   never from the payload).

   **`§3.3` is the clause you will be argued with about, so read it before you
   need it. R-13.** The only sentence the sign gate may claim is **"a commit
   cannot be made without a POST from the authenticated session to
   `/api/sign/{id}/respond`."** A second forgery survives R-1 and is **open
   today** — POST `/respond` yourself with the session cookie, never render the
   dialog, then commit; every rejection code in `erp/contracts/signature.schema.json` `x-rejectionCodes` was walked and none fires,
   inside our own N-04 threat model. R-1 bought something real and it must be
   quoted with its cost: the attacker loses the name and the timestamp, so the
   record is a **true attribution of a false event**, indistinguishable from a
   real click forever. `S5`'s `confirm_token` is **defence in depth, not a
   proof** — it raises cost, it does not establish personhood, and its value is a
   direct function of open unknown **`V3`**. Strike "a commit cannot be made
   without a human decision" on sight, in any draft, from any seat.
4. **`§4 DISCLOSURE` — self-disclosure beats being caught.** The document's
   purpose is to let an expert find the flaws; volunteering a weakness scores
   better than having it discovered. `§4.3` applies this to `§3.3` specifically,
   and that is the disclosure most likely to be talked out of the README at 23:00

## Output format

One block per ruling, delivered to PM **in the message body**. There is no ruling
log and you do not create one; `erp/RUBRIC.md` §5 is the admissibility bar this
block has to clear, and a `NOT ENOUGH` missing any of `CLAUSE`, `BECAUSE`, `FIX`
or `COST` is inadmissible and comes back to you:

```
DATE:     2026-08-31
SUBJECT:  D4 storyboard v2
RULING:   NOT ENOUGH
CLAUSE:   RUBRIC §1.1 — mechanism legible in the first 10 seconds, no narration
BECAUSE:  <=4 sentences, concrete
FIX:      <the smallest change that would flip the ruling>
COST:     <hours, so PM can price it>
```

`RULING` is one of `ENOUGH`, `NOT ENOUGH`, `ENOUGH IF` (with the `IF` spelled out
in `FIX`). No other values.
